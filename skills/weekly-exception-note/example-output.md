## Exceptions

### Cheaper conversions, same number of real enquiries
- **What moved:** Cost per platform conversion £41.80 vs £55.00 baseline (down 24%). Form leads 31 vs 30 and answered calls over 60 seconds 11 vs 12: flat.
- **Evidence:** "Click to call (website)" went from 0 to 38 conversions a week from 3 September, the day the new mobile header went live. The call tracking log shows 11 of those 38 taps became a call over 60 seconds.
- **Likely cause:** The call button on the new mobile header counts every tap as a conversion and it was set as primary. High confidence.
- **Proposed action:** Set "Click to call (website)" to secondary, keep "Calls from ads (60s+)" primary. · **Needs approval:** yes

### Flat Roofs campaign limited by budget

- **Evidence:** Impressions down 18% while impression share lost to rank held at 12%. Demand is there; budget is the limiter.

- **Proposed action:** Run target-box-check on this campaign before changing the target or budget. · **Needs approval:** yes
